FTO Analysis?

FTO, or Freedom to Operate, is a thorough assessment of existing patents in your target market to determine if your product or technology might infringe on any active intellectual property rights. This analysis helps clients avoid costly patent disputes and infringement claims.

Benefits of FTO Analysis

  • Risk Mitigation: Identifies any patent barriers to minimize legal risks.
  • Informed Decision-Making: Provides a clear understanding of the patent landscape.
  • Strategic Market Planning: Helps with choosing regions and timelines for market entry.
  • Cost-Effective Development: Avoids costly redesigns by assessing potential risks early.
